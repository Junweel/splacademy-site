Portuguese–English bridges

Your Portuguese can be a bridge—not a trap.

You may already recognise information and activity. But using “I pretend to…” to describe a plan can change your meaning. Prior knowledge can help; apparent similarities can also mislead.

Find useful patterns

Explore Portuguese–English word families and their exceptions. Patterns are clues, not universal conversion rules.

Check false friends

Try a workplace sentence with words such as actually, pretend, push and library. Receive a hint and compare with a model.

Move from recognition to use

Existing pronunciation drills support clearer speech; a new situation invites your own production. No promise of guaranteed fluency or accent removal.
